Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The app that runs the Instagram account @emma561080 keeps only a small log of comments it has replied to (comment ID, your username as shown in the comment, and the reply text). See the privacy policy for details.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @emma561080 on Instagram with the words "delete my data".
  2. Send it from the Instagram account whose data you want deleted, so we can find the matching records.
  3. We will delete every record related to your account from the app's logs within 30 days and reply to confirm.

You can also delete your own comments on Instagram at any time; the app will not reply to a comment that has been deleted.
